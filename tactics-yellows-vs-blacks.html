Yellows vs Blacks — Formation Options

They control the midfield — so either bypass it or overload it. Two options below.

Option 1 — 3-1-3

Bypass their midfield. Quick balls over the top into three forwards. The lone DM shields the back three and disrupts their rhythm.

Option 2 — 3-2-2

Contest the midfield directly. Two CMs battle for control. More solid but less attacking — good if you need a draw or go a goal up.
